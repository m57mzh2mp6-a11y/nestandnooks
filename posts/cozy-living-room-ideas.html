Cozy Living Room Ideas on a Budget

Living Room Decor • Nest & Nooks

A cozy living room doesn't require a big budget or a full renovation. With the right textures, colors, and a few intentional pieces, you can transform your space into a warm, inviting retreat. Here are our favorite budget-friendly ideas to make your living room feel instantly cozier.
